01 — The Story

Built by engineers tired of “close enough”

Sizr was born from a simple frustration: why does every brand guess at sizes? We're two engineers who believe clothing should fit the way furniture does when it's custom-built — precisely, intentionally, without compromise.

Starting in Pakistan with some of the world's finest textile craftsmanship, we're building an AI-powered system that takes the guesswork out of fit. Our first drop: heavyweight, precision-fit essentials that feel like they were made for you — because they were.
