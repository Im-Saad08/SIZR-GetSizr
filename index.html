01 The story

Built by two engineers who were tired of “close enough.”

It started in a fitting room in Islamabad, watching a perfectly good shirt hang loose across shoulders that didn’t match the pattern.

The fabric was beautiful. The stitching was clean. The tag said Medium. The body inside said this doesn’t work. We asked a question nobody in fashion seemed willing to ask: what if clothing fit the way furniture does when it’s custom-built? Not “close enough.” Not “size up for comfort.” Precisely. Intentionally.

We’re engineers — computer-vision people — and we met at NUTECH, found each other again over a shared hatred of ill-fitted collars. So we built the thing no one had: a fit system that maps the actual body, then cuts the garment to match.

02 The obsessions

Three constraints, refused.

01
Proportion mapping

Why settle for a letter when you can have a measurement?

S/M/L is a statistical approximation from the 1940s. We scan your body — browser-based, no app, no hardware — extract 47 landmarks, and compute your proportion matrix. Clothes fit because they’re built around your actual shape. Every curve · every ratio

02
Material integrity

The cloth is the contract.

300–400 GSM heavyweight fabrics that drape, move and last. We source from Pakistan’s finest mills — the same houses supplying luxury labels in Milan, London and New York. Heavy canvas, real structure, cloth that wears its weight. Sourced · not compromised

03
Method · honest pricing

Premium construction at honest prices.

We cut the middlemen, not the quality. Third-generation tailors in Sialkot, industrial machines calibrated daily, quality control at every station. Every seam is earned — and it’s priced like it. Every stitch intentional

03 The system
Precision-fit system

Fit engineering,
not fashion math.

Traditional grading scales one base pattern up and down. We don’t grade — we generate. Each pattern is a one-off, derived from your scan, adjusted for fabric behaviour, ease preferences and the garment’s intended drape.

  1. I Step 01

    The Scan

    Browser-based computer vision. No app, no special hardware — stand in front of your phone camera for 40 seconds. We extract 47 body landmarks.

    40 seconds · no hardware
  2. II Step 02

    The Matrix

    We compute your proportion matrix — every curve, every ratio, every asymmetry mapped to sub-millimetre precision.

    47 pts → 12 ratios
  3. III Step 03

    The Pattern

    We generate — not grade — a one-off digital pattern from your scan, tuned to fabric behaviour and the intended drape.

    One pattern per body
  4. IV Step 04

    The Atelier

    Cut and sewn in Sialkot, Pakistan by third-generation tailors — the same hands that stitch for global luxury houses.

    Sialkot · PK
